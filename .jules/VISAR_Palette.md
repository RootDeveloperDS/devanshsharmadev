## 2024-05-24 - Improved Interaction Feedback and Focus Visibility
**Learning:** Adding `active:scale-95` to buttons and ensuring text inputs have clear `focus:ring-primary` outlines are extremely cheap, CSS-only ways to dramatically increase the perceived premium quality and accessibility of a site without impacting performance.
**Action:** Always check form elements for `focus:ring-2` visibility and look for main action buttons that lack tactile click feedback via `active:scale`.
