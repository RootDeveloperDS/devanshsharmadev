## 2026-10-14 - Interaction Feedback Optimization
**Learning:** Implementing performant CSS transforms (`group-hover:scale-110`) and tactile feedback (`active:scale-95`) significantly improves the perceived responsiveness of interactive elements without triggering layout shifts caused by animating layout properties like `gap`.
**Action:** Prioritize transform-based animations combined with `transition-all` and `group` utility classes for hover and click states across navigation links and buttons to ensure a smooth, premium feel.
