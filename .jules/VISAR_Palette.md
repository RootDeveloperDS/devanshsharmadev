## 2024-05-20 - Add tactile interactions and transition feedback
**Learning:** Adding simple transition utility classes and tactile interaction utility classes such as `transition-all` and `active:scale-95` to core form components like Input, Textarea, and Button greatly enhances the premium feel and provides more visual interaction feedback to users.
**Action:** Consistently utilize `transition-all` rather than discrete transition properties when practical on base interactive inputs, and `active:scale-95` for buttons.
