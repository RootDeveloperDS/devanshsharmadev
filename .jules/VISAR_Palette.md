## 2024-05-20 - Tactile Interaction Feedback
**Learning:** Adding consistent tactile feedback (`active:scale-[0.98]` or `active:scale-95` combined with `transition-all duration-200`) makes interactive elements like buttons and links feel more premium and physically responsive without relying on complex animations.
**Action:** Always consider adding subtle scale transformations to active states of clickable elements to enhance the perceived responsiveness and premium feel of the interface.
