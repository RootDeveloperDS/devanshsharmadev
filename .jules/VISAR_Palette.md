## 2024-05-06 - Tactile Feedback for Interactive Elements
**Learning:** Adding subtle scaling interactions on click (e.g. `active:scale-95`) makes navigation and links feel substantially more premium without disrupting layout or adding heavy animations.
**Action:** Consistently use `active:scale-95` along with `transition-all` on custom interactive buttons and key links to maintain the "premium, sci-fi" VISAR feel.
