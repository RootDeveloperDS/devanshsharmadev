## 2024-08-08 - Keyboard Focus and Hover States
**Learning:** Proper keyboard focus states (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-primary`) drastically improve accessibility for non-mouse users. Subtle visual transitions (`group-hover:scale-[1.03] duration-500`) combined with `overflow-hidden` elevate a design from standard to premium sci-fi, aligning with the VISAR aesthetic.
**Action:** Always verify `<a>` tags and interactive buttons have clear, branded focus states. Use gentle, duration-based transforms for interaction feedback.
